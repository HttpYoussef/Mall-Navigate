Ticket 01 · palette prototype · react before it locks

MallAR Light Palette

A proposed semantic colour system for MallAR — one set of tokens, light + dark values, every text/UI pair meeting WCAG AA. Below: the one call that needs your eye (the teal), the full token table, the two busiest screens rendered in it, and what happens to the dark “glass/glow” look. This page itself is painted in the proposed palette.

The one decision

Teal, split into three roles

The brand teal #1A8C8C is locked as the accent — but as small text or an icon on white it lands at 4.1:1, under the 4.5:1 AA bar (the inventory already caught CyanGlow text at ~1.2:1 and green at ~2.5:1). Fix: keep #1A8C8C as the brand mark, and use two AA-tuned teals for anything functional.

Purple #9D5CFF (DesignPurple) and the bespoke indigo #5847E8 in DestinationSelectionScreen are both retired — everything routes to the teal roles. Gold #C39D51 is dropped for now (no confident use; can return as a voucher accent later).

React to: is a three-way teal split acceptable, or would you rather one teal everywhere and accept that teal-coloured text can’t be used on light (only teal fills with white text)? The split is the recommendation — it keeps the brand mark intact and makes “teal link” legal.

Recommendation · the “glass / glow” look

Light mode drops the glass

The Home flow leans on translucent dark cards + an animated accent glow. Neither reads on white — a 45%-opacity card over a white ground is just a slightly grey box, and a soft glow on white is invisible. Recommendation: light mode = flat surfaces + one real shadow + the teal on the primary action. Emphasis comes from elevation and the accent button, not light bloom.

Dark mode — keep the glass

  • The infinite glow animations (rememberInfiniteTransition on the hero + search) — disabled in light mode, kept in dark. Also respects reduced-motion.
  • The one exception: a static 6% accent radial wash behind the hero card only — reads as a faint tint, not a glow. Optional; drop it if you want fully flat.
  • StoreLogoContainer stops forcing a white box in dark mode — it uses surface.
React to: flat + elevation for light mode — yes, or do you want to try to preserve a hint of the glow identity?
Recommendation · splash

One brand splash, both modes

Today the splash is hardcoded navy #06131A — not a brand colour. Ticket 06 already decided the splash keeps one fixed background (no values-night). Recommendation: #0F5F5F — the deep brand teal (DarkTeal), logo in white on top. On-brand, works as a bridge into either mode, sub-second.

If this locks

What Ticket 01 hands to Ticket 03

  • The ~26 token names + light & dark hex above become the palette table in docs/Theming/.
  • Ticket 03 maps each M3 ColorScheme role to one of these (primary = accent, onPrimary = onAccent, surface, outline = border, error, the surfaceContainer* ladder to surface/sunken, etc. — no Material purple left).
  • Ticket 08 turns the app-specific ones (accentText, scrim*, *Text status variants, shadow) into the MallColors extension.
  • Open sub-question for Ticket 03: does DestinationSelectionScreen’s bespoke violet system get fully flattened to teal, or keep one distinct hue as a wayfinding cue? Prototype says flatten.
